How it works: rtl_fm FM-demodulates the pager channel; multimon-ng decodes POCSAG512/1200/2400 + FLEX. Pager traffic is unencrypted, so messages come through as plain text.
One dongle: this takes the RTL-SDR from the sub-GHz sweep / ADS-B while running. Legality: pager messages are often sensitive (medical, security) — decode third-party traffic only where lawful.
