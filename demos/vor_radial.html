How it works: rtl_fm AM-demodulates the VOR. The reference 30 Hz is FM-modulated on a 9960 Hz subcarrier (same phase everywhere); the variable 30 Hz is AM'd by the station's rotating pattern (phase = your bearing). Ragnar recovers both and reports their phase difference — your radial. The Cal° box trims a fixed receiver/site offset.
One dongle: this takes the RTL-SDR from the sub-GHz sweep / ADS-B / pager while running. Not for navigation — a hobby SDR + wire antenna is a demonstrator, not a certified nav receiver.
